# agile-templates

Having a clear structure for managing work with tickets is key to staying organized and delivering high-quality results. By properly breaking down our work into Epics, User Stories, Bugs, Tasks, and Subtasks, we ensure that each piece of work is well-defined and manageable.

It's important to take the time to fill out all the required details for each ticket, as this not only helps us now but also serves as a valuable record for future reference—especially when tracking deployments, revisiting past decisions, or managing releases across Salesforce environments.

Each ticket type has a designated owner with clearly defined responsibilities, helping everyone understand their role and scope of work. This structure improves our collaboration, ensures accountability, and keeps the entire team aligned on our goals.

By following this framework, we create a smoother workflow, stronger communication within the team, and a reliable foundation for successful Salesforce delivery.
